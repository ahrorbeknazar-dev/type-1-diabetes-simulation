    About this simulation

    The glucose–insulin model follows the Dalla Man et al. (2007) meal model with UVA/Padova subcutaneous insulin kinetics, calibrated to one virtual 70 kg adult. Glucagon, fatty-acid and ketone parts are simplified. Organ shapes are procedural and molecules are enlarged so you can see them.

    For learning only — not for making treatment or dosing decisions.
